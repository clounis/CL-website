Academic Profile

Research scientist specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Reinforcement Learning, sequential decision-making and Multi-Agent Systems, with applications to Human-AI collaboration and complex aerospace and industrial systems. My research combines algorithmic development, simulation and human-subject experimentation to design adaptive artificial agents able to coordinate with human operators under uncertainty and partial observability.

My interdisciplinary background spans Artificial Intelligence, cognitive science and Human-System Interaction, with experience in scientific leadership, graduate teaching, student supervision and collaborative R&D with academic, aerospace, defence and industrial partners. Current research directions include Human-AI teaming for collaborative production systems, adaptive decision support, mixed-initiative systems and intelligent socio-technical systems.
